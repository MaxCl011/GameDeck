#What I did
I researched what specs and components would be required for playing light games and cloud gaming on the handheld, I decided on a Raspberry Pi CM4 with 4GB of Ram. This should allow me to play retro games, cloud games and maybe even some heavily optimised Minecraft java. Since I want all 4 of the ports (2x USB-A, USB-C, Micro-HDMI) on the top side of the handheld I will need to design a custom carrier board for the CM4. Wiring 2x 5,000mAh batteries in series will allow me to get 10,000mAh in capacity or 5-8 hours of game time, I will also need to design a battery management board in KiCad and a 3d-printable chassis.
